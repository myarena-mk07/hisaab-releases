Hisaab Privacy Policy

Publisher: NEXER_TECH · Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Contact: info@nexertechnology.com

In short: Hisaab keeps your books on your own PC. You need no account. Hisaab sends no telemetry and no analytics. NEXER_TECH runs no server for Hisaab and receives none of your data. Your data leaves your PC only through an online service that you set up yourself, and only to that service.

1. What Hisaab is

Hisaab is an accounting, inventory, GST and payroll program for Windows. This policy applies to the Hisaab app from the Microsoft Store and to the Hisaab setup file from our releases page.

2. Where your data is

  • Your books (companies, ledgers, vouchers, stock, payroll, users) stay on your PC, in Documents\Hisaab\Data: one SQLite file for each company. You can select another folder.
  • Hisaab makes a daily backup of each company in Documents\Hisaab\backups. Other backups go to the folder that you select.
  • Hisaab writes a log of its errors on your PC (%APPDATA%\Hisaab\logs). The log and the backups stay on your PC.
  • Passwords of Hisaab users are stored as one-way hashes (argon2id). Keys, tokens and passwords of the online services that you set up are stored encrypted on your PC.
  • When you turn on Hisaab Vault for a company, Hisaab encrypts that company with your password.

3. What we collect

Nothing. Hisaab has no account, no sign-in to NEXER_TECH, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. NEXER_TECH does not run a server for Hisaab and does not receive your books or any information about how you use Hisaab.

4. When data leaves your PC

Only when you set up and use one of the services below. Then Hisaab sends the data that the service needs, directly from your PC to that service, with your own account at that service. The policy of that service applies to the data that it receives.

Service that you set upWhat goes there
Backup to your own Google Drive or OneDrive (Hisaab Drive), or to a synced cloud folderThe backup file, encrypted on your PC before it leaves (AES-256-GCM, your backup password). The cloud cannot read it.
E-mail (your SMTP server, Gmail or Microsoft 365)The e-mails that you send, with their PDF or Excel attachments.
GST portal, e-Invoice (IRP) and e-Way Bill, directly or through your GST Suvidha ProviderThe returns, invoices and e-way bills that you upload, and the requests that you make.
Your bank (Connected Banking) or an Account AggregatorThe requests for balances and statements, and the payments that you approve.
Payment gateways (Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree)The payment links that you make: amount, invoice number and the customer details that you enter.
WhatsApp (your own WhatsApp Business account)The messages and PDFs that you send. The Windows share window sends only what you select, through the app that you select.
Remote access through a relayThe relay forwards only encrypted data between your office PC and the remote user (end-to-end encrypted; the relay cannot read it).
Branch sync with your other Hisaab serverThe changes of the company, signed and encrypted with your sync key, to your own other server.
AI reading in Hisaab Docs (optional, off by default)When you turn it on with your own key, the document that you give Hisaab Docs goes to Anthropic for reading. Without it, Hisaab reads documents on your PC.
Switch from TallyNothing leaves your PC or office network: Hisaab reads the companies from your own Tally.
A support file that you send to us (F1 > Troubleshooting > Save a Support File)Hisaab makes the file on your Desktop and sends nothing. When you attach it to an e-mail to us, we receive the error log, the versions and the settings without passwords; company data only when you tick it. We use it only to help you.

5. Updates

The Microsoft Store installs the updates of the Store copy. The setup-file copy checks our public releases page on GitHub for a new version; that check sends no data about you or your books.

6. Your control

  • You can turn off or remove every online service in Hisaab Services at any time.
  • You can delete your data: delete the company in Hisaab, or delete the folder Documents\Hisaab. Removing the app does not delete your books, so that you do not lose them by mistake.
  • Requests about data at a service that you set up go to that service.

7. Children

Hisaab is a business program. It is not made for children and collects no data from anyone.

8. Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
